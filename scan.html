Scan your house with LiDAR

Walk through room by room and Home.io finds the walls, doors and windows for you

The short version: lights on, doors open, one room at a time, move slowly, and tap Next Room while you stand in the doorway. Do a few rooms per sitting, not the whole house.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad with LiDAR: an iPhone Pro or Pro Max (12 Pro or newer), or an iPad Pro from 2020 or newer. On other devices Home.io doesn't offer scanning; you can draw the rooms or import a model there.
  • A charged device. Scanning works the phone hard and it gets warm.
  • You only scan once. The plan syncs to your other devices through iCloud, so borrow a Pro phone for the scan if yours has no LiDAR.

Before you start

  • Turn the lights on and open the curtains. The scanner loses its place in a dim room.
  • Open the doors between rooms so the doorways are found.
  • Clear the floor along the walls where you can. Walls hidden behind piles of things come out crooked.
  • Plan your route. Start in one room and work outward to the rooms next to it.

Scan the first rooms

  1. Open Floor Plans and tap Scan Rooms with LiDAR.
  2. Stand in the first room and point the camera at a wall. Turn slowly on the spot, then walk the edge of the room. Tilt down to catch where the walls meet the floor and up to where they meet the ceiling. White lines appear as each wall, door and window is found.
  3. When the room's outline has closed, walk to the doorway of the next room and tap Next Room. Home.io saves the room you finished, so a problem later costs you one room, not the house.
  4. Scan the next room the same way. The line at the bottom keeps count: "2 Rooms saved."
  5. Tap Done after the last room. Home.io puts the rooms together and opens your new plan.

Keep Home.io on screen the whole time. Switching to another app or locking the phone stops the scan.

How much to scan at once

Apple's scanner is built for a room or a few, about 2,000 square feet (190 m²) at most in one go. Past that it can run out of room or the phone can overheat. For a big house, stop after a few rooms and come back for the rest.

Scan More Rooms (Home.io 1.0.9 and later) adds another sitting to the plan you already have. It's in Floor Plans and in the plan's menu in the sidebar. You pick the floor the new rooms belong on, or a new level above or below, then tap Start Scanning. Adding to a floor you have is free; a new level is part of Home.io Pro.

Each sitting is set down beside the rooms already there, so nothing has to line up while you scan. To put a sitting where it really is, switch to Plan, tap Move Scan, then drag it into place and turn it.

More than one story

Home.io 1.0.9 and later. If you keep scanning up or down the stairs, each story comes out as its own floor, and the one you started on is the 1st Floor. Without Pro, the floor you started on is the one you get. For a big house, one story per sitting with Scan More Rooms is the safer way.

After the scan

  • Match Rooms lines up the scanned rooms with your rooms in Apple Home or Home Assistant, so your accessories land in the right place. Rooms are matched by name where the names agree.
  • Rooms open to each other, like a kitchen and dining room with no wall between, come out as one room named for both. In Match Rooms › Rooms on the Model, Join merges two rooms yourself.
  • Edit Model is where you rename rooms, set levels, paint and add furniture.

When a scan goes wrong

Home.io tells you why it stopped and keeps the rooms you had finished. Tap the Use Rooms button (it shows how many, like "Use 3 Rooms") to keep them, or Scan Again to redo the room that failed.

  • "Your device lost track of where it was." It was too dark, you moved too fast, or the camera faced a blank wall or a window for too long. Lights on, slow down, and keep some furniture or corners in view.
  • "That was too much for one scan." Finish with what you have, then add the rest with Scan More Rooms, a smaller area at a time.
  • "Your device got too hot." Let it cool for a few minutes, out of its case if it has one.
  • "The scan stopped when you left Home.io." Keep the app on screen until you tap Done.
  • A wall, door or window is missing or crooked. Mirrors, glass doors and very dark walls confuse the scanner. Rescan that room slowly, or fix it afterward in Edit Model.

Send a scan report

If a scan keeps failing or comes out wrong, open Floor Plans and tap Send Scan Report. It shares a small file that says how your last scan went and the shapes of its rooms. It has no photos. Send it to jibjabsmash@gmail.com with a line about what looked wrong, and it gets fixed from that.

No LiDAR, or it just won't scan?

You can still get your house in. In Floor Plans, Draw a Floor Plan lets you trace each room on a grid, or you can import a Sweet Home 3D file or a 3D model. The support page shows how to have an AI assistant build a model from your floor plan.
