Home.io

Your home in 3D, live · for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Home.io puts your Apple Home and Home Assistant accessories on a 3D model of your own house. See which lights are on, which doors are open and how warm each room is at a glance, and tap anything to control it.

What it does

  • Your house in 3D or as a plan, with every light, lock, door, sensor, camera and appliance where it really is. Tap to control it.
  • Tiles: dashboards you arrange yourself, with accessories, scenes, rooms, cameras, the weather, security, energy and timers.
  • An idle screen for an iPad on the wall: the house or a dashboard, the time, weather, what's playing, and severe weather alerts. It can wake when someone walks up.
  • Schedules at a time, at sunrise or sunset, or when a door opens, saved in Apple Home or Home Assistant. With Pro: only at night, only when someone's home, only if a door is closed, and back off again after a while.
  • Energy, Device Health and History: electricity use and cost, low batteries and accessories that stopped answering, and temperature, humidity and battery charts.
  • Real weather on the model: the sun where it really is, rain, snow that builds up on the roof, trees that bend in a storm, and lightning.
  • Backups of your plans, settings and your Apple Home setup (rooms, names, scenes, automations), automatically every week to iCloud Drive.

Free and Pro

Home.io is free to download, with one floor plan of your own (drawn, from photos, scanned or imported) with one floor, Tiles with one dashboard, schedules, and everything for controlling and watching your home. Home.io Pro is a single purchase, no subscription, shared with your family: every floor and any number of plans, the model editor, Replay, cameras on the model, smarter automations, more dashboards, History, and restoring Apple Home from a backup.

Make a 3D model of your house with AI

You can have Claude (or another AI assistant) make a model of your house for Home.io:

  1. Copy the prompt below and paste it into Claude, with a photo of your floor plan if you have one.
  2. Answer its questions. It writes a script that builds your house.
  3. On a Mac or PC, install Blender (free). Open it, choose the Scripting tab, click New, paste the script and click Run Script. MyHouse.usdz appears on your Desktop.
  4. Put the file in iCloud Drive (or AirDrop it), then in Home.io open Floor Plans and tap Import 3D Model.

I'd like a 3D model of my house for the Home.io app, which shows my smart home on a 3D model of the house. Please write one Python script for Blender (version 4 or newer) that builds the house and saves it as a USDZ file.

What the model needs:
• Real size, in metres. The ground floor's floor surface at height 0, each floor above about 3 m higher (measured to its floor surface), and a basement's floor about 2.7 m below.
• On each floor: a floor slab under every room, outside walls about 0.15 m thick and inside walls about 0.1 m, about 2.6 m high, with door openings (about 0.9 × 2.1 m) cut through the walls, stairs where they are, and windows as openings with a thin pane of glass.
• No ceilings, so each floor can be seen from above. The floor of the storey above sits on the walls below.
• A roof over the top floor in my house's shape, with eaves about 0.3 m past the walls.
• Porches, decks and a garage if I have them. No furniture, lights, cameras, trees, ground or lawn: Home.io adds its own.
• Simple materials named for what they are: "Outside walls", "Roof", "Floors", "Inside walls", "Trim", "Glass".
• At the end, select everything and export it with bpy.ops.wm.usd_export to a file called MyHouse.usdz on my Desktop.

Before you write the script, ask me for what you need: how many floors, each room's name, size and place, where the doors, windows and stairs are, the roof's shape, and which side the front door is on. I can give you a photo or sketch of my floor plan, a listing's floor plan, or measurements.

Common questions

My accessories aren't in the right rooms. Rooms in Apple Home or Home Assistant are matched to your plan by name. Tap the orange note in the sidebar, or open an accessory and pick its room under Room on Plan.

How do I connect Home Assistant? Tap your plan's name at the top of the sidebar, choose Connect Home Assistant, and paste a long-lived access token (Home Assistant → your profile → Security).

Can I control Home Assistant away from home? Yes. Leave an iPad at home with Home.io open and turn on Use This iPad as the Home Hub. Your iPhone then works through your own iCloud, with no network setup.

How do I make a dashboard? Choose Tiles at the top (next to 3D and Plan) and tap Make One for Me, or Start Empty and add your own tiles. Edit lets you add, remove, resize and drag tiles.

How do I use an iPad on the wall? In Settings, turn on Wall Mount Mode and choose when the idle screen comes on and what it shows (the house, one of your saved views, or a dashboard).

Why is the history empty for an Apple Home sensor? Apple Home keeps no history, so Home.io keeps the readings itself while it's open. An iPad left on as a home hub collects the most. Home Assistant sensors show Home Assistant's own history.

Can lightning on the model follow real strikes? Yes, if Home Assistant has a lightning sensor: the free Blitzortung integration, or a weather station with a lightning detector. Otherwise it flashes during thunderstorms reported by Apple Weather.

Restoring Home.io Pro. Open any Pro feature and tap Restore Purchase.
